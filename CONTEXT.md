# Cassette Player — Context

**Rack**:
The cassette rack window that holds the album history. Each album that is loaded or dropped on it gets a spine on the shelf.
_Avoid_: shelf window, library

**Spine**:
One album's cassette-box spine in the rack: a strip of the album cover with "Artist - Album (Year)" written along it. Single songs never get a spine.
_Avoid_: tile, card

**Sort mode**:
The ordering of the rack: Recent (by when each album was last loaded) or Custom (the order the owner dragged the spines into).
_Avoid_: view, filter

**Preview**:
The read-only tracklist that slides out of a spine on a single click in the rack.
_Avoid_: popup, peek, details

**Mixtape**:
A folder whose tracks do not all share one artist. Its track rows read "01. Title", the same as an album's.

**Now playing**:
The album currently in the deck. Its spine is softly lit in the **Rack**.
_Avoid_: active, selected

**Resume point**:
The track and time an album returns to when its **Spine** is double-clicked: where it was last paused, or the start of the track it was stopped on. An album never played starts at track 1, 0:00.
_Avoid_: bookmark, saved session

**Boost**:
Extra gain on the music for quiet tracks: Off, +3, +6, +9 or +12 dB. It is limited so it does not clip, and it is separate from the volume knob, which only lowers the level.

**Cassette design**:
The look of the cassette in the deck for one album: one of eight colour sets (black, blue, cream, grey, pastel green, red, white, yellow). An album gets one at random the first time it reaches the **Rack** and keeps it until the owner picks another. Single songs and an empty deck show the original cassette.
_Avoid_: skin, theme, tape colour

**Album edit**:
The owner's customisation of one album: its cover image, which part of the cover the **Spine** shows, an optional solid spine colour, the spine text and its colour, and the **Cassette design**. Made in the Album Edit window and kept with the album's **Rack** entry.
_Avoid_: album settings, metadata edit

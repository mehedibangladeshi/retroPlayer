# Cassette Player — Context

**Rack**:
The cassette rack window that holds the album history. Each album that is loaded or dropped on it gets a spine on the shelf.
_Avoid_: shelf window, library

**Spine**:
One album's cassette-box spine in the rack: a strip of the album cover with "Artist - Album (Year)" written along it. Single songs never get a spine.
_Avoid_: tile, card

**Sort mode**:
The ordering of the rack: Recent (by when each album was last loaded) or Custom (the order the owner dragged the spines into).
_Avoid_: view, filter

**Preview**:
The read-only tracklist that slides out of a spine on a single click in the rack.
_Avoid_: popup, peek, details
